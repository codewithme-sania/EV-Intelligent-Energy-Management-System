# Problem Statement

## Project Title

Intelligent Energy Management System for Electric Vehicles

## Background

Electric vehicles use battery energy to power their propulsion systems. Their power demand changes according to driving conditions, making energy management an important aspect of EV system design.

## Problem

An energy management strategy needs to account for changing power demand and available battery energy. This project explores a MATLAB-based approach to studying this challenge.

## Objectives

* Study energy management challenges in electric vehicles.
* Investigate battery energy utilization.
* Develop an approach for managing EV energy demand.
* Evaluate system behavior through testing and simulation when implemented.

## Scope

The project focuses on an energy management approach for electric vehicles using MATLAB. Specific implementation details and verified results will be documented as they become available.

## Current Status

MATLAB screenshots and project documentation are being organized in this repository. Executable source code and verified simulation results are not currently included.
